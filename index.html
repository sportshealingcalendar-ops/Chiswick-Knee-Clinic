How an appointment works

  1. History and examination. A structured clinical assessment of the knee, hip and gait.
  2. Imaging where it changes the plan. Weight-bearing X-ray or MRI requested only when the result would alter management.
  3. A written plan. Options explained with their expected benefits, risks and recovery time, so the decision is yours.

Before you book

This site is general information, not clinical advice, and it is not a substitute for a consultation. If your knee gives way, locks, or is hot and swollen with a fever, seek urgent medical attention.
