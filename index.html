Meet your surgeon

Mr Chinmay Gupte

Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon · Clinical Reader (Associate Professor) in knee & trauma surgery, Imperial College London

Your knee is treated by Mr Chinmay Gupte, a consultant knee and trauma surgeon at Imperial College London and director of the Wellington Knee Unit, the UK's first dedicated knee clinic. He treats both adults and children, and his research at Imperial feeds directly into how he operates. Book with him as your knee specialist in Chiswick and he sees you personally at every visit — read his full profile and results.
