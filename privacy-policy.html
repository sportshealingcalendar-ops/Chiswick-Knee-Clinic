What we collect

When you send an appointment request we collect your name, date of birth, phone number, email address and the reason for your appointment.

Why we hold it

We use these details only to respond to your enquiry and to arrange your care. We do not sell them and we do not share them for marketing.

How long we keep it

Clinical records are kept for the period set out in NHS and independent sector retention schedules. Enquiries that do not lead to an appointment are deleted sooner.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
